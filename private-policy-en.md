# Said Keyboard Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

You can use Said and its keyboard on iPhone and iPad without an account. Said recognizes speech, adds punctuation and line breaks, and suggests corrections on your device. Said does not upload your audio or text to us or to third parties. We do not use that content to train models. The app has no third-party advertising or behavioral analytics services and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Microphone and dictation

After you grant microphone access and tap the microphone button on the keyboard, the Said app starts listening. When you switch back to the app you’re writing in, Said continues listening in the background for that session. To stop, tap the microphone button again or tap the stop button in Said. Said processes the audio in memory for speech recognition and does not save an audio file.

If your device is low on memory or Said cannot load its preferred speech model, Said uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition. Your device connects to Apple when it needs to download or update a speech or punctuation model. Apple may process connection details such as your IP address to deliver the download. The download request does not include your audio or text. See [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).

## The keyboard and Allow Full Access

The keyboard reads the text that the current input field makes available, including your selection and the text on either side of the cursor. It uses this context to insert dictated text at the cursor, replace incorrect characters, and adjust quotation marks and line breaks.

Turning on **Allow Full Access** lets the keyboard and the Said app share the status of the current dictation session, the relevant text, and the dictation results on your device. The keyboard also reads the current app’s identifier on your device so Said can switch back to that app after dictation starts.

## Data retention and deletion

- Said and its keyboard keep a **dictation session record** in shared storage on your device. After dictation ends and the keyboard confirms it has inserted the text, it deletes the record. If the session is interrupted, the record may remain until the keyboard clears it or a new session replaces it.
- The app you write in manages **the text you enter**, including any copies it syncs to the cloud. To remove that text, edit or delete the document in that app.
- Said keeps **settings and error details** on your device to remember your dictation preferences and show the cause of the most recent dictation error. Choosing **Delete App** removes Said’s settings and session data from that device.

## Revoking permissions

You can turn off **Microphone** at any time in **Settings → Apps → Said**. On that same screen, open the keyboard settings to turn off **Allow Full Access** or disable the Said keyboard. Turning off full access prevents the keyboard from using the Said app for dictation.

## Contact the developer

If you have a privacy question or want to access, correct, or delete information you’ve sent us by email, contact us at [said.app@icloud.com](mailto:said.app@icloud.com).

We receive your email through iCloud Mail. We use your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send only to handle and respond to your request. We keep the correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request and any necessary follow-up. We require service providers that process personal information on our behalf to provide the same level of protection described in this policy.
